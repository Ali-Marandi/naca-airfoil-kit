Objections Drive the Follow-Up, Not a Feature Roadmap

In this illustrative set, budget appears 3 times; trust/accuracy, privacy, scope mismatch, and timing also appear.
Each objection is linked to a follow-up action rather than an immediate engineering task.
This workflow prevents a small sample size from becoming an unsupported “top customer request” claim.
Budget
Verify budget owner, purchase cycle, and baseline workflow cost.
Privacy / Security
Clarify data-minimization policy and deployment boundaries.
Scope Mismatch
Redefine the job-to-be-done or reduce package size; do not promise features.
Trust / Accuracy
Review screening-only scope and the validation evidence package.
