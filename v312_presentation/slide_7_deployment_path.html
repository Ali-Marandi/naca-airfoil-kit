Deployment: Demo to Operations

Scaling infrastructure investment with actual market traction.
Streamlit Community Cloud
Best For
Public demos, training, and zero-install access.
Boundary
No external worker, operational secrets, or SLA guarantees in this layer.
Internal Worker + Kubernetes
Best For
Controlled solver execution, policy enforcement, observability, and scale.
Boundary
Requires digest pinning, secret rotation, gateway TLS/mTLS, and CNI enforcing.
Production Platform
Best For
Enterprise customers requiring strict SLAs and dedicated resources.
Boundary
SLO, monitoring, backup, capacity, and incident processes need clear ownership.
The public product is demonstrable independently of the production solver, and the worker is only activated within a controlled internal boundary.
