Commercial Roadmap

Moving from screening to an evidence-backed design workspace.
0–3 Months
Priority
XFOIL worker staging, NACA 0012 validation pack, and monitoring.
Exit Criteria
Reproducible report & policy test passed in CNI enforcing environment.
3–6 Months
Priority
Multi-condition optimization with thickness, moment, and manufacturing constraints.
Exit Criteria
Mission-based objectives & comprehensive sensitivity report.
6–12 Months
Priority
Enterprise workspace, private studies, collaboration, and audit governance.
Exit Criteria
Access control, storage policy, and lifecycle study management.
The roadmap is not focused on claiming more features; it is focused on increasing evidence fidelity and reducing adoption risk. Every stage has a measurable technical exit criterion.
